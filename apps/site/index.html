Why local-first

A studio that answers to you.

Keys stay on your machine

API keys live in an encrypted store on your own local backend. Each key is sent only to the provider it belongs to, and is never logged.

Works fully offline

Point it at a local ComfyUI backend and unplug. A built-in mock provider runs the whole app with no key and no account at all.

Layers you can trust

Stack, reorder, mask, 16 blend modes — all non-destructive. AI actions see exactly what the canvas shows: hide part of a layer, and the model never gets it.

Editing

The edits you actually make.

Inpaint

Paint a mask yourself, or let it derive one from the subject.

Outpaint

Grow the picture past its own edges.

Upscale

More pixels, same picture, one click.

Background removal

Runs in your browser on WebGPU. Nothing leaves the machine.

Remix & variations

Riff on any layer without touching the original.

No lock-in

Leaves as real files.

Export a flat PNG, or an OpenRaster .ora with every layer, mask, opacity and blend mode intact. Open it elsewhere and keep working.
